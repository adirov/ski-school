1c:Tc22,<h2 id="in-short">In short</h2>
<p>Weather in the mountains above Almaty changes faster than in the city: hot in the sun, cold in the shade and wind. So the main rule is layers you can easily take off and put on. Below is a list for a normal resort day and a separate list for off-piste riding.</p>
<h2 id="clothing-three-layers">Clothing: three layers</h2>
<div class="table-wrap"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Layer</th><th scope="col">What works</th><th scope="col">What to avoid</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Base</td><td>Synthetic or merino wool thermal underwear</td><td>A cotton T-shirt</td></tr><tr><td>Mid</td><td>A fleece or light down jacket</td><td>A thick jumper that does not dry</td></tr><tr><td>Outer</td><td>Waterproof membrane jacket and trousers</td><td>Jeans and a city jacket</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
<p>Add warm socks (one pair, not two: a second pair squeezes your foot in the boot), a neck gaiter or balaclava, and a thin hat under the helmet if the helmet is loose.</p>
<h2 id="protection">Protection</h2>
<ul>
<li>A helmet is a must.</li>
<li>Ski goggles: on sunny days the snow is blinding, and on cloudy days goggles help you see the terrain.</li>
<li>Impact shorts and knee pads for beginners.</li>
<li>Wrist guards while you are learning.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="small-things-people-forget">Small things people forget</h2>
<ul>
<li>High-SPF sunscreen and lip balm: UV is stronger at altitude.</li>
<li>Water and a snack.</li>
<li>A power bank: phones drain faster in the cold.</li>
<li>Spare gloves.</li>
<li>Cash and a card.</li>
<li>An ID document for rental.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="for-children">For children</h2>
<p>Children need the same layers, plus spare mittens and socks. Check that the helmet fits and is fastened. We discuss the details of kids&#39; sessions with parents when booking.</p>
<h2 id="for-off-piste-days">For off-piste days</h2>
<p>Riding outside groomed runs comes with avalanche risk. On top of the usual list you need:</p>
<ul>
<li>an avalanche transceiver (beacon), shovel and probe for every rider;</li>
<li>the skills to use them, practised in advance rather than on the day;</li>
<li>a backpack that fits this gear;</li>
<li>a guide who knows the area and follows the avalanche situation.</li>
</ul>
<p>Before you go, check the weather forecast, Kazhydromet&#39;s report on snow conditions in the mountains and any avalanche warnings from the Ministry of Emergency Situations. The guide makes the final call on the route on the spot.</p>
<h2 id="checklist-before-you-leave-home">Checklist before you leave home</h2>
<ul>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"> Three layers of clothing with a waterproof outer layer</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"> Helmet and goggles</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"> Gloves and a spare pair</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"> Sunscreen and lip balm</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"> Water, snack, power bank</li>
<li><input disabled="" type="checkbox"> ID and money</li>
</ul>
<p>Questions about gear or the lesson format? <a href="/en/contact/">Ask us</a>.</p>
