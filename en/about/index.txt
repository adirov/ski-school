1b:T86f,<h2 id="in-short">In short</h2>
<p>Almaty Snowboard School has been teaching snowboarding in Almaty since 2005. Lessons are taught by certified instructors with 10 to 40 years of experience at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka resorts. A 2-hour private lesson costs 60,000 KZT, a group lesson 40,000 KZT per person. Contact: Artur Kovalevskiy, +7 777 525 98 85 (phone and WhatsApp).</p>
<p>We help you start from scratch, improve your technique or go off-piste with a guide.</p>
<h2 id="what-we-offer">What we offer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Private lessons</strong>: the instructor works only with you, at your pace and level. 1.5 hours: 45,000 KZT; 2 hours: 60,000 KZT; half day (3 hours): 120,000 KZT; full day (6 hours): 240,000 KZT; night skiing lesson (2 hours): 80,000 KZT.</li>
<li><strong>Group lessons</strong>: a 2-hour lesson with an instructor for 2–5 people, 40,000 KZT per person.</li>
<li><strong>Kids training</strong>: for children from age 5, 1.5-hour sessions, one-to-one or in a group. Price on request.</li>
<li><strong>Guided off-piste riding</strong>: a full-day outing for experienced riders, with the route chosen by snow and weather. Price on request.</li>
</ul>
<p>See all prices on the <a href="/en/prices/">Prices page</a>.</p>
<h2 id="where-lessons-take-place">Where lessons take place</h2>
<p>Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty: <strong>Shymbulak</strong>, <strong>Ak-Bulak</strong>, <strong>Oi-Karagai</strong> and <strong>Lesnaya Skazka</strong>. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office; rental gear is paid separately, and the instructor helps you choose a board and boots.</p>
<h2 id="how-we-work">How we work</h2>
<ol>
<li>You call us, message us on WhatsApp or send a request with your dates, level and group size.</li>
<li>We suggest a format and confirm the instructor, time and meeting point.</li>
<li>A booking is confirmed only once we reply to confirm it.</li>
</ol>
<p>Mountain weather changes fast. For safety the instructor may adjust the program or move a session; we agree on this in advance.</p>
