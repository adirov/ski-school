17:T1005,<p>This document is an official proposal (public offer) from IE &quot;KAZAKHSTAN TRAVEL AGENCY&quot;, under which Almaty Snowboard School operates (hereinafter — the &quot;Provider&quot;), to any individual or legal entity (hereinafter — the &quot;Customer&quot;) that accepts this offer under the conditions below.</p>
<p>In accordance with Article 395 of the Civil Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, acceptance of this offer is the Customer making a partial or full prepayment via an individual link (invoice) that the Provider sends to the Customer by e-mail or messenger.</p>
<h2 id="1-subject-of-the-agreement">1. Subject of the agreement</h2>
<p>1.1. At the Customer&#39;s request, the Provider runs private and group snowboard lessons, kids training and guided off-piste riding at ski resorts near Almaty, and the Customer pays for these services under the terms of this Agreement.</p>
<p>1.2. Lesson prices are published on the <a href="/en/prices/">Prices</a> page. The ski pass (lift ticket) and equipment rental are not included in the lesson price unless agreed otherwise.</p>
<h2 id="2-booking-and-payment">2. Booking and payment</h2>
<p>2.1. A request by phone, WhatsApp or through the website is an enquiry. Lesson prices are listed on the Prices page (/en/prices/) and depend on the format, length and day of the week; the ski pass is not included. The date, time, resort, meeting point and format are agreed with the Customer before confirmation.</p>
<p>2.2. After agreement, the Provider generates a personal payment link (electronic invoice) via the TipTop Pay payment system and sends it to the Customer.</p>
<p>2.3. To confirm the booking and secure the instructor&#39;s time, the Customer makes a 30% prepayment via the link within 3 banking days of receiving it.</p>
<p>2.4. The remaining 70% is paid in cash on the day of the lesson before it starts.</p>
<p>2.5. Non-cash payments are accepted in tenge (KZT). International guests pay the invoice with Visa/Mastercard in their own currency, converted at their bank&#39;s rate.</p>
<h2 id="3-rights-and-obligations-of-the-parties">3. Rights and obligations of the parties</h2>
<p>3.1. The Provider shall: run the lesson at the agreed time and in the agreed format; provide a qualified instructor or guide; explain the safety rules on the slope.</p>
<p>3.2. The Customer shall: give accurate contact details, the age and riding level of participants, and tell the instructor about injuries or health conditions; follow the instructor&#39;s directions and the resort rules; wear a helmet in lessons. Kids&#39; lessons require a parent&#39;s consent.</p>
<h2 id="4-cancellation-and-refunds">4. Cancellation and refunds</h2>
<p>4.1. If the Customer cancels, the prepayment is refunded minus the Provider&#39;s actual expenses that cannot be cancelled.</p>
<p>4.2. If the Customer cancels less than 7 days before the lesson, the non-cash prepayment is retained by the Provider as a penalty for the instructor booking.</p>
<p>4.3. Prepayments are refunded only to the card used for the payment via TipTop Pay, within 5–10 banking days. No refund applies to the cash portion if it has not yet been paid.</p>
<h2 id="5-weather-safety-and-force-majeure">5. Weather, safety and force majeure</h2>
<p>5.1. Safety comes first. The instructor or guide may change, shorten or reschedule a lesson in poor visibility, strong wind, lift closures or avalanche danger. Off-piste riding runs only in suitable conditions; the guide decides.</p>
<p>5.2. The parties are exempt from liability for non-performance caused by force majeure: natural disasters, avalanche danger, closure of the resort, runs or roads by the resort administration or government order.</p>
