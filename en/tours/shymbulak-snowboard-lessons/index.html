Snowboard and ski lessons at Shymbulak

Private and group snowboard and ski lessons with a certified instructor at Shymbulak, Almaty's main ski resort above Medeu.

Shymbulak is the closest large ski resort to the city: it sits above Medeu in the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains. It has gentle learning slopes for first lessons and long runs for riders who already ride.

Almaty Snowboard School runs private and group snowboard and ski lessons at Shymbulak. The instructor builds the lesson around your level, from your first stance on a board or skis to confident turns and technique on steeper runs.

A private lesson costs 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours or 60,000 KZT for 2 hours, a half day (3 hours) 120,000 KZT, a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT and a 2-hour night skiing lesson 80,000 KZT. A 2-hour group lesson for 2–5 people costs 40,000 KZT per person. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office.

Included

  • A certified instructor for the whole booked time.
  • A lesson plan built around your level and goal.
  • Help choosing rental gear if you do not have your own.

Not included

  • The ski pass: guests buy it themselves at the Shymbulak ticket office.
  • Board, boot and helmet rental.
  • Getting to the resort.

FAQ

Is Shymbulak good for a first snowboard lesson?

Yes. Shymbulak has gentle learning slopes where beginners learn to stand on the board, stop and make their first turns. A 2-hour private lesson with an Almaty Snowboard School instructor costs 60,000 KZT, a group lesson 40,000 KZT per person. The ski pass and rental are paid separately. We agree the time and meeting point when you book.

Is the Shymbulak ski pass included in the lesson price?

No. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the Shymbulak ticket office; it is not included in the Almaty Snowboard School lesson price. The lesson price covers a certified instructor for the whole booked time and help choosing rental gear. Check current ski pass prices on the resort's official website.
