About Almaty Snowboard School

In short

Almaty Snowboard School has been teaching snowboarding in Almaty since 2005. Lessons are taught by certified instructors with 10 to 40 years of experience at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka resorts. A 2-hour private lesson costs 60,000 KZT, a group lesson 40,000 KZT per person. Contact: Artur Kovalevskiy, +7 777 525 98 85 (phone and WhatsApp).

We help you start from scratch, improve your technique or go off-piste with a guide.

What we offer

  • Private lessons: the instructor works only with you, at your pace and level. 1.5 hours: 45,000 KZT; 2 hours: 60,000 KZT; half day (3 hours): 120,000 KZT; full day (6 hours): 240,000 KZT; night skiing lesson (2 hours): 80,000 KZT.
  • Group lessons: a 2-hour lesson with an instructor for 2–5 people, 40,000 KZT per person.
  • Kids training: for children from age 5, 1.5-hour sessions, one-to-one or in a group. Price on request.
  • Guided off-piste riding: a full-day outing for experienced riders, with the route chosen by snow and weather. Price on request.

See all prices on the Prices page.

Where lessons take place

Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty: Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office; rental gear is paid separately, and the instructor helps you choose a board and boots.

How we work

  1. You call us, message us on WhatsApp or send a request with your dates, level and group size.
  2. We suggest a format and confirm the instructor, time and meeting point.
  3. A booking is confirmed only once we reply to confirm it.

Mountain weather changes fast. For safety the instructor may adjust the program or move a session; we agree on this in advance.

Almaty Snowboard School

Almaty Snowboard School is a snowboard and ski school in Almaty, teaching since 2005: private and group snowboard and ski lessons, kids' sessions from age 5 and guided off-piste riding. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka resorts.
