Frequently asked questions

What is Almaty Snowboard School?

Almaty Snowboard School is a snowboard school in Almaty. We run private lessons, group courses, kids training and guided off-piste trips in the Trans-Ili Alatau mountains.

Is a request on the site a booking?

No. A request is an enquiry. We contact you to confirm dates, level and terms. A booking is confirmed only when we reply with a confirmation.

How do I find out the price?

The price depends on the format, length, dates and group size. Call us or send a request and we will quote a price before you book.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Beginners start with an instructor on a gentle learning slope. See our guide on how to start snowboarding in Almaty.

Private lesson or group course: which should I choose?

A private lesson is a 2-hour one-to-one session with an instructor: good if you want to progress faster or feel nervous on the slope. A group course is 5 sessions of 2 hours in a group, handy if you are learning with friends. All formats are compared on the Lessons page.
