Private snowboard lessons

One-to-one lessons with an instructor, from your first day or to improve your technique.

The instructor works only with you, so pace and drills follow your level and goal.

Beginners usually start with stance, stopping and first turns on a gentle slope. If you already ride, the lesson can focus on one goal: linked turns, speed or confidence on steeper runs.
