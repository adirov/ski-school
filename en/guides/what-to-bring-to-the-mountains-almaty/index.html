What to bring to the mountains of Almaty in winter: a snowboarder's checklist

A checklist of clothing, protection, documents and small items for a day on the slopes near Almaty, plus a separate list for off-piste days.

Author
Almaty Snowboard School editorial team
Published

Short answer

For a day of riding in the mountains near Almaty, bring layered clothing (thermal base layer, fleece, waterproof shell), a helmet, goggles, two pairs of gloves, sunscreen, water, a snack and a charged phone. For off-piste riding you also need an avalanche transceiver, shovel and probe, the skills to use them and a guide.

In short

Weather in the mountains above Almaty changes faster than in the city: hot in the sun, cold in the shade and wind. So the main rule is layers you can easily take off and put on. Below is a list for a normal resort day and a separate list for off-piste riding.

Clothing: three layers

LayerWhat worksWhat to avoid
BaseSynthetic or merino wool thermal underwearA cotton T-shirt
MidA fleece or light down jacketA thick jumper that does not dry
OuterWaterproof membrane jacket and trousersJeans and a city jacket

Add warm socks (one pair, not two: a second pair squeezes your foot in the boot), a neck gaiter or balaclava, and a thin hat under the helmet if the helmet is loose.

Protection

  • A helmet is a must.
  • Ski goggles: on sunny days the snow is blinding, and on cloudy days goggles help you see the terrain.
  • Impact shorts and knee pads for beginners.
  • Wrist guards while you are learning.

Small things people forget

  • High-SPF sunscreen and lip balm: UV is stronger at altitude.
  • Water and a snack.
  • A power bank: phones drain faster in the cold.
  • Spare gloves.
  • Cash and a card.
  • An ID document for rental.

For children

Children need the same layers, plus spare mittens and socks. Check that the helmet fits and is fastened. We discuss the details of kids' sessions with parents when booking.

For off-piste days

Riding outside groomed runs comes with avalanche risk. On top of the usual list you need:

  • an avalanche transceiver (beacon), shovel and probe for every rider;
  • the skills to use them, practised in advance rather than on the day;
  • a backpack that fits this gear;
  • a guide who knows the area and follows the avalanche situation.

Before you go, check the weather forecast, Kazhydromet's report on snow conditions in the mountains and any avalanche warnings from the Ministry of Emergency Situations. The guide makes the final call on the route on the spot.

Checklist before you leave home

  • Three layers of clothing with a waterproof outer layer
  • Helmet and goggles
  • Gloves and a spare pair
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Water, snack, power bank
  • ID and money
