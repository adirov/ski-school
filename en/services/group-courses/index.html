How a group lesson goes

The group format suits people who want to learn to snowboard or ski with friends or family: the instructor leads the group and picks drills for its overall level.

Groups are 2 to 5 people, a lesson lasts 2 hours and costs 40,000 KZT per participant. The ski pass is bought separately. We confirm available dates in reply to your request.

Ski passes (lift tickets) and equipment rental are charged separately: guests buy the ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office, and the instructor helps you choose rental gear.

Where lessons take place

Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book.

  • Shymbulak — Almaty's main ski resort above Medeu. Good both for first lessons and for long runs.
  • Ak-Bulak — A resort in the Talgar gorge. A calm setting for learning and family trips.
  • Oi-Karagai — Oi-Karagai ski resort near Almaty, one of the school's four teaching venues.
  • Lesnaya Skazka — Lesnaya Skazka resort in the mountains near Almaty: you can book a lesson with a school instructor here too.

What the price includes

  • A 2-hour lesson with a certified instructor in a group of 2 to 5 people.
  • Drills for the group's level: for beginners and for those who already ride green and blue runs.
  • Help choosing rental gear if you do not have your own.

What you pay for separately

  • The ski pass: each participant buys it at the resort ticket office.
  • Board, boot and helmet rental if you do not have your own gear.
  • Getting to the resort. Our partner agency almaty-travel.kz can arrange a transfer.

How your first group lesson goes

The group meets the instructor at the resort, at the point we confirm when you book. The instructor helps anyone without gear choose it at the rental shop.

Beginners start on a gentle learning slope: stance, stopping on an edge, the "falling leaf" slide and first turns. If the group already rides, the lesson focuses on linked turns and confidence on the run.

If you want more of the instructor's attention, or the participants ride at different levels, choose a private lesson: 2 hours cost 60,000 KZT.

Questions

How much is a group snowboard or ski lesson in Almaty?

A group snowboard or ski lesson at Almaty Snowboard School lasts 2 hours and costs 40,000 KZT per participant. Groups are 2 to 5 people, so a lesson for three comes to 120,000 KZT. Everyone buys their own ski pass at the resort ticket office, and rental gear is paid separately too. Booking: +7 700 525 98 85.

How big are the groups and who are they for?

Almaty Snowboard School groups have 2 to 5 people. Group lessons are for beginners and for riders who already handle green and blue runs. A lesson lasts 2 hours, and the instructor picks drills for the group's overall level. It works best when you learn with friends or family.

Where do group lessons take place?

Almaty Snowboard School group lessons take place at resorts near Almaty: Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. We agree the resort, time and meeting point with the instructor when you book by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85. Each participant buys the ski pass at the resort ticket office.
