## In short

Almaty Snowboard School has been teaching snowboarding in Almaty since 2005. Lessons are taught by certified instructors with 10 to 40 years of experience at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka resorts. A 2-hour private lesson costs 50,000 KZT, a group lesson 25,000 KZT per person. Contact: Artur Kovalevskiy, +7 700 525 98 85 (phone and WhatsApp).

We help you start from scratch, improve your technique or go off-piste with a guide.

## What we offer

- **Private lessons**: the instructor works only with you, at your pace and level. 2 hours: 50,000 KZT; half day (3 hours): 65,000 KZT on weekdays and 75,000 KZT at weekends; full day (6 hours): 130,000 KZT.
- **Group lessons**: a 2-hour lesson with an instructor for 2–5 people, 25,000 KZT per person.
- **Kids training**: for children from age 5, 1.5-hour sessions, one-to-one or in a group. Price on request.
- **Guided off-piste riding**: a full-day outing for experienced riders, with the route chosen by snow and weather. Price on request.

See all prices on the [Prices page](/en/prices/).

## Where lessons take place

Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty: **Shymbulak**, **Ak-Bulak**, **Oi-Karagai** and **Lesnaya Skazka**. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book. Guests buy a half-day or full-day ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office; rental gear is paid separately, and the instructor helps you choose a board and boots.

## How we work

1. You call us, message us on WhatsApp or send a request with your dates, level and group size.
2. We suggest a format and confirm the instructor, time and meeting point.
3. A booking is confirmed only once we reply to confirm it.

Mountain weather changes fast. For safety the instructor may adjust the program or move a session; we agree on this in advance.
