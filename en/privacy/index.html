Privacy policy

1. Who processes your data

Your data is processed by Almaty Snowboard School. For any data question, call or message the number on the Contact page.

This policy follows the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

2. How the booking form works

The form on this site does not send or store anything on our server. When you press the button, your browser opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message: your name, the chosen service, date, group size, riding level and comment. The message is sent only when you press "Send" in WhatsApp yourself.

From then on the conversation happens in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp (Meta) processes it under its own terms. We see your message, name and phone number in WhatsApp, as in any chat. Please do not include health details unless they matter for the session.

If you call or message us directly, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us.

3. Why we need it

To answer your request, suggest a format and confirm your booking. We do not send marketing without your separate consent.

4. Technical data

Like any web server, the hosting server automatically logs the IP address, request time, page address and browser details. These logs keep the site running and secure; we do not use them to identify you.

Fonts and images load from our own domain. There are no third-party counters or advertising scripts on the site.

5. Analytics and cookies

Web analytics is not enabled on the site right now, and the site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If we enable it, it will start only after you agree in the consent banner, and your name, phone number and message will never be sent to it. We will update this page when that happens.

6. Who receives your data

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising. Only the school staff who answer requests see the conversation.

7. Storage and deletion

We keep conversations for as long as needed to reply and run the session. To ask us to delete your data, use the contact in section 1.
