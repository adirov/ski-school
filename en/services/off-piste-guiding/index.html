Guided off-piste riding

Riding beyond groomed runs for experienced riders.

Level
Advanced
Format
Private or group
Duration
Full day
Price
on request

How the day goes

Off-piste riding carries avalanche risk and needs experience, equipment and a guide. The route is chosen by snow, weather and the group’s ability.

We agree on required equipment, the route and weather rescheduling with you before the trip.

Book a lessonAsk a question

Where lessons take place

Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book.

  • Shymbulak — Almaty's main ski resort above Medeu. Good both for first lessons and for long runs.
  • Ak-Bulak — A resort in the Talgar gorge. A calm setting for learning and family trips.
  • Oi-Karagai — Oi-Karagai ski resort near Almaty, one of the school's four teaching venues.
  • Lesnaya Skazka — Lesnaya Skazka resort in the mountains near Almaty: you can book a lesson with a school instructor here too.

Who it is for

  • Experienced riders who are confident on red and black runs.
  • Riders who want powder in the Almaty mountains with a guide rather than alone.
  • If you only ride groomed runs so far, start with a private lesson: the instructor will get you ready for off-piste.

Equipment

  • An avalanche transceiver, a shovel and a probe for every rider, and the skills to use them.
  • A helmet, goggles and layered clothing with a waterproof shell.
  • A backpack, water and food for a full day.
  • We agree what gear you need and where to get it with you before the trip.

Safety and weather

The guide chooses the route by snow, weather and the group's ability and makes the final call on every descent. If avalanche danger is high or the weather is bad, we move the outing to another day agreed with you.

Questions

Can a beginner go off-piste?

No. Guided off-piste riding at Almaty Snowboard School is for experienced riders who are confident on red and black runs. You need an avalanche transceiver, a shovel and a probe and the skills to use them, and the guide makes the final call on the route. If you only ride groomed runs so far, start with a private lesson with an instructor.

How much does guided off-piste riding in Almaty cost?

The price of guided off-piste riding depends on the route, group size and equipment, so we quote it on request by WhatsApp or phone on +7 777 525 98 85. An outing takes a full day. If the route starts from a resort lift, guests buy the ski pass themselves. We agree the route and weather rescheduling terms before the trip.
