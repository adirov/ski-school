Snowboard and ski lesson prices in Almaty

Private and group lessons with an instructor for beginner and advanced skiers and snowboarders. Prices in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT), per lesson or per person.

A private snowboard or ski lesson with an instructor in Almaty costs 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours and 60,000 KZT for 2 hours. A half day (3 hours) is 120,000 KZT, a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT and a 2-hour night skiing lesson 80,000 KZT. A 2-hour group lesson for 2 to 5 people is 40,000 KZT per person. The ski pass and rental gear are paid separately. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka.

Questions about prices

How much is a private snowboard lesson in Almaty?

A 2-hour private lesson with an Almaty Snowboard School instructor costs 60,000 KZT, and a 1.5-hour lesson 45,000 KZT. A half day (3 hours) is 120,000 KZT and a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT. A 2-hour night skiing lesson is 80,000 KZT. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office, and rental gear is paid separately too.

How much is a group lesson?

A group snowboard lesson at Almaty Snowboard School lasts 2 hours and costs 40,000 KZT per participant. Groups are 2 to 5 people: for example, a lesson for two costs 80,000 KZT and for five 200,000 KZT. Each participant pays for their own ski pass and rental gear. We confirm available dates by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85.

What does the lesson price include?

The Almaty Snowboard School lesson price covers a certified instructor for the whole booked time: 2, 3 or 6 hours. The instructor helps you choose rental gear. It does not include the ski pass, which guests buy themselves at the resort ticket office, rental gear or getting to the resort.
