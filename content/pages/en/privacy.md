## 1. Who processes your data

Your data is processed by the Almaty Snowboard School snowboard school. For any data question, call the number on the [Contact](/en/contact/) page.

This policy follows the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection".

## 2. What data we receive

When you send a request: your name, contact (phone, messenger or email), the direction you are interested in, preferred date, group size, riding level and comment. Please do not include health details in the comment unless they matter for the session.

Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to protect the form from spam and is not stored with the request.

## 3. Why

To answer your request, suggest a format and confirm your booking. We do not send you marketing without separate consent.

## 4. Who receives the data

Requests go to the school’s work channel, where only the staff who answer requests can see them. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising.

## 5. Analytics and cookies

If web analytics is enabled, it starts only after you agree in the consent banner. Your name, phone number and other request details are never sent to analytics.

## 6. Storage and deletion

We keep requests for as long as needed to reply and run the session. You can ask us to delete your data using the contact in section 1.
