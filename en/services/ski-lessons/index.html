How ski lessons go

Almaty Snowboard School teaches more than snowboarding: the school's instructors also give ski lessons to beginners, to skiers who already ride and to children from age 5. The school has been teaching in Almaty since 2005, and its instructors have 10 to 40 years of experience.

In a first ski lesson the instructor shows you the stance, the snowplough stop and your first turns on a gentle learning slope. If you already ski, we help you move on to parallel turns, ride blue and red runs with confidence and go faster without tension.

A private ski lesson costs 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours and 60,000 KZT for 2 hours, a half day (3 hours) 120,000 KZT, a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT and a 2-hour night skiing lesson 80,000 KZT. A 2-hour group lesson for 2 to 5 people is 40,000 KZT per person. The ski pass and rental are paid separately.

Where lessons take place

Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book.

  • Shymbulak — Almaty's main ski resort above Medeu. Good both for first lessons and for long runs.
  • Ak-Bulak — A resort in the Talgar gorge. A calm setting for learning and family trips.
  • Oi-Karagai — Oi-Karagai ski resort near Almaty, one of the school's four teaching venues.
  • Lesnaya Skazka — Lesnaya Skazka resort in the mountains near Almaty: you can book a lesson with a school instructor here too.

Who the lessons are for

  • Adults who have never skied and want to learn calmly and safely.
  • Skiers who still snowplough and want to move to parallel turns.
  • Confident skiers who want technique work on steeper runs or a night skiing session with an instructor.
  • Friends and families: a group lesson for 2 to 5 people when everyone is at a similar level.

What the price includes

  • A certified ski instructor for the whole booked time.
  • A plan built around your level, from your first steps on skis to technique on red runs.
  • Help choosing skis, boots and poles at the rental shop if you have no gear of your own.

What you pay for separately

  • The ski pass: guests buy it themselves at the resort ticket office.
  • Ski, boot, pole and helmet rental.
  • Getting to the resort. Our partner agency almaty-travel.kz can arrange a transfer.

Questions

How much is a ski lesson with an instructor in Almaty?

A private ski lesson with Almaty Snowboard School costs 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours and 60,000 KZT for 2 hours. A half day (3 hours) is 120,000 KZT, a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT and a 2-hour night skiing lesson 80,000 KZT. A 2-hour group lesson is 40,000 KZT per person, for groups of 2 to 5. The ski pass and rental are paid separately.

Can an adult learn to ski from scratch?

Yes. Most adult students start with no experience. In the first lesson an Almaty Snowboard School instructor teaches you to stand on skis, stop with a snowplough and turn on a gentle slope. A 1.5 to 2-hour private lesson is a good start, as the pace follows you.

Where do the ski lessons take place?

Ski lessons take place at four resorts near Almaty: Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book by phone or WhatsApp on +7 777 525 98 85.

Skiing or snowboarding: which is better for a beginner?

Skiing is usually easier on day one: standing and stopping come quicker. Snowboarding is harder at first, but many people link turns within a few lessons. At Almaty Snowboard School you can learn either at the same prices; if you are unsure, message us and an instructor will advise.
