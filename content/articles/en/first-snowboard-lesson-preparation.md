## In short

Your first lesson goes more smoothly when your body is ready for the effort, your clothes stay dry and your equipment is sorted before the lesson starts. Here is a day-by-day plan.

## A week before: get your body ready

Snowboarding works your thighs, glutes, core and ankles. If you have not exercised for a while, spend 10–15 minutes a day for 5–7 days before the trip on:

- squats and lunges;
- planks on straight arms and on forearms;
- standing on one leg with your eyes closed;
- a light hamstring stretch.

The goal is not to build muscle but to keep your legs from tiring out in the first hour.

## The evening before: clothes and documents

Pack in layers: a thermal base layer, a warm mid layer and a waterproof jacket and trousers. Avoid cotton: it gets wet and makes you cold. Bring a spare pair of gloves or mittens.

Check that you have:

- an ID document (rental shops may ask for one);
- a charger or power bank: phones drain faster in the cold;
- sunscreen and lip balm;
- ski goggles or sunglasses.

The full list is in our article ["What to bring to the mountains of Almaty"](/en/guides/what-to-bring-to-the-mountains-almaty/).

## In the morning: breakfast and time

Eat a proper breakfast: you burn energy faster in the cold and at altitude. Bring water and a snack. Leave enough time for the journey, the rental queue and changing, so you do not start the lesson tired and stressed.

## Rental: how to tell the gear fits

| What to check | How it should be |
|---|---|
| Boots | Your heel does not lift when you lean forward; toes lightly touch the front |
| Board length | For a beginner, usually between chin and nose height |
| Bindings | They close without force and the straps are not twisted |
| Helmet | It does not wobble when you tilt your head and does not press |

If something rubs or hurts, say so at the rental shop straight away, not an hour later on the slope.

## What to tell your instructor

A good instructor will ask, but it helps to mention up front:

- whether you have tried snowboarding, skiing, skateboarding or surfing;
- any knee, back or wrist injuries;
- what scares you: speed, lifts, falling;
- what you want to achieve by the end of the day.

## During the lesson

Drink water, take short breaks and say honestly when you are tired. Mistakes and falls on day one are normal. Getting your stopping right matters more than rushing into turns.

## After the lesson

Do a light stretch in the evening and get enough sleep. If you plan a second day in a row, expect sore muscles. Do not ignore sharp joint pain or a blow to the head: see a doctor.
