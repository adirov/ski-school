How kids' sessions go

Snowboard and ski sessions for children with an instructor: a calm pace, clear drills and attention to safety.

We teach children from 5 years old, and a session lasts an hour and a half. We agree on the format (private or in a kids group) with parents when you book.

Book a lessonAsk a question

Where lessons take place

Lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty. We agree the resort, time and meeting point when you book.

  • Shymbulak — Almaty's main ski resort above Medeu. Good both for first lessons and for long runs.
  • Ak-Bulak — A resort in the Talgar gorge. A calm setting for learning and family trips.
  • Oi-Karagai — Oi-Karagai ski resort near Almaty, one of the school's four teaching venues.
  • Lesnaya Skazka — Lesnaya Skazka resort in the mountains near Almaty: you can book a lesson with a school instructor here too.

What to bring for your child

  • A helmet: it is required in lessons. If you do not have one, rent it at the resort.
  • A warm waterproof jacket and pants, thermal underwear and a fleece.
  • Goggles, and a hat or balaclava that fits under the helmet.
  • Spare mittens and socks: children get wet quickly.
  • Water and a small snack.

What you pay for separately

  • The ski pass: guests buy it themselves at the resort ticket office.
  • Board, boot and helmet rental if your child has no gear of their own.
  • Getting to the resort.

How to book a kids' session

Message us on WhatsApp or call +7 700 525 98 85. Tell us your child's age, whether they have ridden before and the dates that suit you. We will suggest the format (one-to-one or a kids' group), the resort and time, and give you the price.

Questions

From what age can a child learn to snowboard?

Almaty Snowboard School teaches children from age 5. A session lasts an hour and a half. We agree the format, one-to-one or in a kids' group, with parents when you book. The price of a kids' session is on request by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85. Lessons take place at resorts near Almaty, and the ski pass is bought separately.

Do you teach children to ski?

Yes. At Almaty Snowboard School children from age 5 learn both snowboarding and skiing. A session lasts an hour and a half, one-to-one or in a kids group. The price is on request by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85. Skis, boots and a helmet in your child's size can be rented at the resort.

Does my child need their own snowboard?

No, a child does not need their own snowboard for the first sessions. A board, boots and a helmet in your child's size can be rented at the resort; rental is paid separately. An Almaty Snowboard School instructor helps choose the gear before the session. Parents should bring warm waterproof clothes, goggles and spare mittens. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the resort ticket office.
