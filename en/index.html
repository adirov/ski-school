Frequently asked questions

What is Almaty Snowboard School?

Almaty Snowboard School is a snowboard school in Almaty that has been teaching since 2005. Certified instructors with 10 to 40 years of experience run private lessons (from 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours), group lessons (40,000 KZT per person), sessions for children from age 5 and guided off-piste riding. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. Booking: +7 700 525 98 85.

Do you teach skiing too?

Yes. Almaty Snowboard School teaches both snowboarding and skiing: private and group lessons for beginner and experienced skiers at the same prices as snowboarding, from 45,000 KZT for a 1.5-hour private lesson and 40,000 KZT per person in a group. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka.

How much does a snowboard or ski lesson cost?

A 2-hour private snowboard lesson with an instructor in Almaty costs 60,000 KZT, and a 1.5-hour lesson 45,000 KZT. A half day (3 hours) is 120,000 KZT and a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT. A 2-hour night skiing lesson is 80,000 KZT. A 2-hour group lesson for 2 to 5 people is 40,000 KZT per person. The ski pass and rental gear are not included. See the Prices page for the full list.

Where do the snowboard lessons take place?

Almaty Snowboard School lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty: Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. We agree the resort, time and meeting point with the instructor when you book by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the chosen resort's ticket office; it is not included in the lesson price.

How long is a snowboard lesson?

A private lesson lasts 1.5 or 2 hours, a half day 3 hours and a full day 6 hours; a night skiing lesson lasts 2 hours. A group lesson for 2 to 5 people lasts 2 hours. A kids' session for children from age 5 lasts an hour and a half. Guided off-piste riding is a full-day outing for experienced riders. Almaty Snowboard School confirms the length and start time when you book.
