Frequently asked questions

Answers to the main questions about snowboard and ski lessons in Almaty: prices, resorts, lesson length, gear, age, booking and transfers.

What is Almaty Snowboard School?

Almaty Snowboard School is a snowboard school in Almaty that has been teaching since 2005. Certified instructors with 10 to 40 years of experience run private lessons (from 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours), group lessons (40,000 KZT per person), sessions for children from age 5 and guided off-piste riding. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. Booking: +7 700 525 98 85.

Do you teach skiing too?

Yes. Almaty Snowboard School teaches both snowboarding and skiing: private and group lessons for beginner and experienced skiers at the same prices as snowboarding, from 45,000 KZT for a 1.5-hour private lesson and 40,000 KZT per person in a group. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka.

How much does a snowboard or ski lesson cost?

A 2-hour private snowboard lesson with an instructor in Almaty costs 60,000 KZT, and a 1.5-hour lesson 45,000 KZT. A half day (3 hours) is 120,000 KZT and a full day (6 hours) 240,000 KZT. A 2-hour night skiing lesson is 80,000 KZT. A 2-hour group lesson for 2 to 5 people is 40,000 KZT per person. The ski pass and rental gear are not included. See the Prices page for the full list.

Where do the snowboard lessons take place?

Almaty Snowboard School lessons take place at four ski resorts near Almaty: Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka. We agree the resort, time and meeting point with the instructor when you book by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85. Guests buy the ski pass themselves at the chosen resort's ticket office; it is not included in the lesson price.

How long is a snowboard lesson?

A private lesson lasts 1.5 or 2 hours, a half day 3 hours and a full day 6 hours; a night skiing lesson lasts 2 hours. A group lesson for 2 to 5 people lasts 2 hours. A kids' session for children from age 5 lasts an hour and a half. Guided off-piste riding is a full-day outing for experienced riders. Almaty Snowboard School confirms the length and start time when you book.

How experienced are the instructors?

Almaty Snowboard School lessons are taught by certified instructors with 10 to 40 years of experience. The school has been teaching snowboarding in Almaty since 2005, from your first day on a board to technique on steep runs and guided off-piste riding. We match an instructor to your level and goal when you book by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85.

Is a request on the site a booking?

No. A request on the site or a WhatsApp message is an enquiry, not a booking. We get in touch to check your dates, level and group size, and suggest a format, instructor, resort and meeting time. Prices are published on the Prices page. A booking is confirmed only when we reply with a confirmation and the prepayment is made under the booking terms.

Is the ski pass included in the lesson price?

No. Guests buy the ski pass (lift ticket) themselves at the resort ticket office: at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai or Lesnaya Skazka. The Almaty Snowboard School lesson price covers the instructor: 2 hours cost 60,000 KZT one-to-one or 40,000 KZT per person in a group. Rental gear is paid separately too.

Do I need experience to start?

No. A beginner starts with an instructor on a gentle learning slope: stance, stopping, the "falling leaf" sideways slide and first turns. For a first time, choose a 2-hour private lesson (60,000 KZT) or a 2-hour group lesson (40,000 KZT per person, groups of 2 to 5). See our guide on how to start snowboarding in Almaty for more.

Private or group lesson: which should I choose?

A private lesson is one-to-one with an instructor: 2 hours for 60,000 KZT. It suits you if you want to progress faster, feel nervous on the slope or work on a specific technique. A group lesson is 2 hours in a group of 2 to 5 people for 40,000 KZT per person: handy if you learn with friends or family, and cheaper. All formats are compared on the Lessons page.

Can I go freeriding without experience?

No. Guided off-piste riding is for experienced riders who are confident on red and black runs. You need an avalanche transceiver, shovel and probe and the skills to use them, and the guide makes the final call on the route. If you only ride groomed runs so far, start with a private lesson.

Do I need to buy equipment?

No, you do not need to buy a board before your first lessons: the main Almaty resorts have rental shops. An Almaty Snowboard School instructor helps you choose a rental board and boots for your level, and we tell you what else to bring when you book. Rental is paid separately from the lesson. A helmet is a must during the lesson.

What is the minimum age?

Almaty Snowboard School teaches children to snowboard from age 5. A kids' session lasts an hour and a half, and we agree the format, one-to-one or in a kids' group, with parents when you book. The price of a kids' session is on request: +7 700 525 98 85. Adults take private lessons (from 45,000 KZT for 1.5 hours) or group lessons (40,000 KZT per person).

Can you help with an airport transfer and sightseeing?

Yes. Airport meeting, transfers to the resorts and tours around Almaty are arranged by our partner travel agency almaty-travel.kz: www.almaty-travel.kz/en/pages/26.html. Snowboard lessons themselves are booked directly with Almaty Snowboard School by phone or WhatsApp on +7 700 525 98 85. The transfer is not included in the lesson price.

Almaty Snowboard School

Almaty Snowboard School is a snowboard and ski school in Almaty, teaching since 2005: private and group snowboard and ski lessons, kids' sessions from age 5 and guided off-piste riding. Lessons take place at Shymbulak, Ak-Bulak, Oi-Karagai and Lesnaya Skazka resorts.
